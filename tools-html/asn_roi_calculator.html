How this tool works

What it does

It estimates the first-year value of membership from two sources, partner discounts and Expert Hotline answers, and subtracts the founding fee so you see a net figure and a multiple of cost.

What to gather before you start

  • Monthly vendor spend: only the categories a listed partner could realistically replace
  • Realistic discount: the member deal you would actually use, not the best case
  • Hotline questions per year: real problems you would send in, not a guess at capacity

The math, in plain English

Partner savings = monthly spend × 12 × discount. Hotline value = questions × $150. Net benefit = partner savings + Hotline value − $588. The multiple = (partner savings + Hotline value) ÷ $588. Every input is yours; the $150 per question and the $588 fee are the only fixed assumptions, and both are shown above.
