How this tool works

What it does

It shows how much revenue a higher same-day close rate would add, per month and per year, without changing how many consults you run or what you charge.

What to gather before you start

  • Consults per month: from your booking system, a typical month
  • Same-day close rate: consults that booked or paid the same day, divided by all consults
  • Average ticket: total treatment revenue divided by closed consults
  • Lift to test: a realistic target in percentage points; 5 to 10 points is a common first goal

The math, in plain English

Current revenue = consults × close rate × average ticket. Target revenue = consults × (close rate + lift) × average ticket. Added revenue = the difference, per month; multiply by 12 for the year.
