How this tool works

What it does

It turns three numbers you already know, cost per unit, price per unit and units per treatment, into profit per treatment and a margin percentage.

What to gather before you start

  • Cost per unit: your real invoice cost per unit, including waste and the injection kit
  • Price per unit: what you actually charge after promotions or memberships
  • Units per treatment: a typical treatment for the area you are pricing

The math, in plain English

Revenue = price per unit × units. Product cost = cost per unit × units. Profit = revenue − product cost. Margin = profit ÷ revenue. Per-unit profit = price per unit − cost per unit.
