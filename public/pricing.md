# Pricing: Aesthetic Success Network

Membership pricing for aesthetic practice owners, experts and partners.
Machine-readable companion to the visual page at
https://www.aestheticsuccessnetwork.com/pricing.

All prices in USD. Cancel anytime: no contracts, no retention call.

## Founding Member

- **Price**: $49/month OR $490/year (pay for 10 months, get 12, save $98/year)
- **Availability**: first 100 members only
- **Rate lock**: locked for as long as the membership stays active
- **Includes**:
  - The Expert Hotline: leave a voicemail, get a written action plan plus
    3 to 4 recommended experts by text and email within 2 to 3 business
    days (AI-assisted, not live, not 24/7)
  - Full resource library access: a growing library of expert kits
    across Pricing & Margins, Consult & Conversion, Team & Culture,
    Patient Experience, Marketing & Growth, and Operations & Compliance,
    with new kits added weekly
  - Every member-only partner deal from vetted, Verified Partners
  - Monthly live AMAs and CE
  - Proven systems, SOPs, templates and checklists
  - 30-day money-back guarantee

## Standard Member

- **Price**: $199/month OR $1,990/year
- **Availability**: open enrollment after the founding cap
- **Rate lock**: none; the standard rate may adjust over time
- **Includes**: the same membership as above (Hotline, resource library,
  partner deals, monthly live AMAs and CE, systems and templates,
  30-day money-back guarantee)

There is no intermediate tier between Founding and Standard.

## Partner Pricing (Provider Agreement)

Companies serving aesthetic practices apply at
https://www.aestheticsuccessnetwork.com/partners. Same Featured Partner
benefits at every phase.

- **Months 1 to 6**: $0/month (180-day trial)
- **Months 7 to 12**: $49/month
- **Month 13 onward**: $199/month or $1,990/year
- **Referral**: $50 per referred member, paid after their first payment
- **Includes (every phase)**: profile and placement in your category,
  lead flow with a dashboard, Verified Partner badge, inclusion in the
  member-only deals program, co-marketing opportunities across the
  network
- **Commitments**: a genuine member-only offer at least as good as any
  offer to comparable customers, Hotline participation when a member
  fits, a working calendar link

## Expert Pricing (Provider Agreement)

Coaches, consultants and educators serving aesthetic practices apply at
https://www.aestheticsuccessnetwork.com/experts. Same Featured Expert
benefits at every phase.

- **Months 1 to 6**: $0/month (180-day trial)
- **Months 7 to 12**: $49/month
- **Month 13 onward**: $199/month or $1,990/year
- **Course revenue split**: experts keep **70%** of net paid-course
  revenue; the network keeps 30% and runs the platform (checkout,
  hosting, refunds)
- **Referral**: $50 per referred member, paid after their first payment
- **Includes (every phase)**: a done-for-you content library produced
  from one recording, in your branding; featured profile; warm leads to
  your calendar; Hotline referrals when a member fits your expertise;
  the ability to sell your own paid courses

Both experts and partners sign the single ASN Provider Agreement:
https://www.aestheticsuccessnetwork.com/agreement/provider

## Refund policy

- **Money back**: 30-day money-back guarantee from the first charge
- **Process**: email hello@aestheticsuccessnetwork.com or use the
  billing portal from your account page
- **Details**: https://www.aestheticsuccessnetwork.com/legal/refund

## Cancellation

- Two-click cancel from your account page (Manage subscription)
- No retention call, no minimum term
- The founding rate is released on cancel; re-joining starts at the
  then-current standard rate

## Billing details

- **Payment processor**: Stripe
- **Cards accepted**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- **Invoices**: auto-generated PDF on every charge, available from the
  account page
- **Currency**: USD only
